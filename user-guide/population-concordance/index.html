    Anchored guideline concordance¶

    Concordance measured from documentation

    Concordance here is measured from documentation in the notes through each patient's anchor note. A step that was done but not documented there counts as not met, and a step the records cannot settle is unknown and left out of the fraction. Guideline spaces are chosen by TrialSpace and the decision model, and workup results come from LLM summaries, extraction and compiled queries: review them against the source notes. The results are research aids, not a measure of the quality of care, and need clinical review.

    matchminer_ai.population also runs a population-level guideline-concordance review without the dashboard. Each patient is anchored at one note written by their provider. The review then reads only the notes up to that anchor:

    1. Anchor. A seeded random note by the patient's provider (in their department, within an optional window).
    2. Prepare (part 1). A summary and structured extraction records from the notes through the anchor.
    3. Assess (part 2). The patient's most relevant guideline space (TrialSpace and the decision model). Then that space's workup items, checked by the compiled structured-extraction queries (by default) or another method.
    4. Report. Concordance per patient and item, per patient, and by provider and department.

    Part 2 runs on part 1's saved outputs, so one prepared population can be assessed again, for example with another method or catalog. The population workup review is the related cohort-level tool: it checks one guideline space across a cohort.

    Inputs¶

    Patients, one row per patient:

    • patient_id;
    • provider_id and/or provider_name (at least one per row);
    • optionally department_id and/or department_name.

    Notes:

    • patient_id, note_date, note_text and optionally note_type;
    • optionally note_id. Without it, each note gets the extraction stages' code-derived id (a hash of patient, date, type and text; patients.extraction.notes.note_ids), so evidence links back to the note;
    • the note's provider_id and/or provider_name, and department_id and/or department_name, as needed to match the patients table.

    Tables are DataFrames, or CSV (read as text) or Parquet files. columns (--columns FIELD=COLUMN on the command line) renames the caller's columns; a key patients.FIELD or notes.FIELD applies to one table only, for example notes.provider_id=AUTHOR_ID.

    Anchor notes¶

    choose_anchor_notes(patients, notes, start_date=None, end_date=None, seed=23) returns (anchors, exclusions).

    Which notes are candidates:

    • Provider. The note's provider must match the patient's: by ID when the patient row gives one, otherwise by name, ignoring case and repeated spaces.
    • Department. When the patient row gives a department, the note must be in it, matched the same way.
    • Window. The note must be dated, with start_date <= note_date <= end_date. Each bound is optional and inclusive, and a bound without a time covers its whole day.

    Choice. The anchor is random.Random(f"{seed}:{patient_id}")'s choice among the candidates in date and note-id order, so it depends on the seed and the patient, not on row order.

    Exclusions. A patient without a candidate is excluded, with one reason:

    Reason Meaning
    no_notes No notes
    no_provider_note No note by the provider
    no_department_note No note by the provider in the department
    no_dated_note No dated note by the provider
    no_note_in_window No note by the provider in the window

    Notes through the anchor (notes_through_anchor). Every note of the patient, by any author, dated on or before the anchor date. When both the note and the anchor carry a time, timestamps are compared, and a note at the anchor's timestamp is included. Undated notes are left out.

    anchors has patient_id, the patient row's provider and department fields, anchor_note_id, anchor_date, anchor_time (None when the anchor note has no time), candidate_notes, notes_through_anchor and undated_notes.

    Part 1: prepare¶

    prepare_anchored_population(patients, notes, output_dir=..., start_date=..., end_date=..., seed=..., summary_config=..., extraction_config=...) runs three stages into one folder:

    Stage What it does Outputs
    anchors/ Anchors, exclusions and the notes through each anchor anchors.parquet, exclusions.parquet, notes.parquet
    summaries/ summarize_patients on those notes summaries.parquet (patient_id, provider and department fields, anchor_date, cancer_history_summary, general_exclusion_criteria_evidence, note_count), qc.parquet
    extractions/ extract_patient_records, then extract_document_records linked to them store/: an extraction record store (document_records.parquet, patient_records.parquet, manifest.json)

    Stages and resuming:

    • Each stage writes a path-free stage_receipt.json last. Its identity holds the input file stamps (size and modification time, never hashes), the window, the seed, the models and the prompt contracts.
    • A complete stage is skipped. An interrupted one resumes from its checkpoints: the summaries' checkpoint folder is keyed by the stage identity, which includes the anchors receipt, and the extractors check their own.
    • A folder begun under other settings is refused; use a fresh one.
    • check=True (--check) reports each stage without running anything: 0 complete, 10 not yet, 20 settings or inputs differ.

    Options:

    • levels="document" extracts document records only.
    • reuse_documents names an extraction checkpoint folder. Its document records of the same notes are reused when their input hash and contract match, which is the extractor's own rule. Records linked to different patient records do not match.

    Which records the dashboard can open. The store's format is the dashboard's (matchminer_ai.patients.extraction.store). Its mode is synthetic with synthetic=True and real otherwise.

    Every note through an anchor is sent to the summary and extraction endpoints, which must be authorized for the notes.

    Part 2: assess¶

    assess_concordance(prepared, catalogs=..., output_dir=..., method="extraction_queries") reads part 1's folder.

    Match. retrieve_guideline_considerations(summaries, catalog, top_n=1, candidate_k=20) runs over the catalogs: TrialSpace's candidate_k nearest spaces get the decision model's match questions, and the space ranked first by match_quality_score is the patient's.

    • Catalogs. By default, the latest complete, audited catalog of each disease under the site's catalog root (trials.latest_guideline_catalogs, the dashboard's rule). The root is the site config's dashboard.guideline_catalogs, else <data root>/no_phi/space_paradigms_nccn.
    • No reasonable match. A patient whose top space's P(reasonable) (match_quality_reasonableness) is below min_reasonableness (default 0.5) is excluded with the reason no_reasonable_guideline_match. The candidate and its scores are kept in matches/guideline_matches.parquet.
    • Reuse. The match stage has its own receipt (matches/). Guideline vectors are cached in the output's guideline_embeddings/ (guideline text only) unless embedding_cache_dir names a shared folder.

    Assess. Patients are grouped by their space. The space's workup items are checked, without the items that ask only for a history and physical (patients.workup.is_history_and_physical). The space's key criteria are the population context, and each patient's evaluation date is the anchor date. Methods:

    Method What it does
    extraction_queries (default) compile_workup_queries once per item and population, then code runs the queries on the patient's extraction records. Queries are cached under query_cache/writer-<model hash>/, the dashboard's layout; query_cache names a shared folder
    extraction_sql The SQL agent explores a database of the run's own records (sql/exploration.duckdb); its accepted queries then run once over each space's patients. Exploration results (extracted values, no ids or note text) reach the query-writing endpoint. Accepted queries keep aggregate counts, so a shared query_cache follows the outputs' PHI rule
    full_notes review_population_workup on each patient's notes through the anchor
    note_search review_patient_workup_with_note_search on the same notes

    For the extraction methods, a patient without any complete or partial extraction record is excluded (no_extraction_records). A patient matched to a space with no workup items left is excluded too (no_workup_items).

    Status mapping¶

    Each result keeps its raw status and reason, and maps to one category (concordance_category):

    Category Compiled queries and SQL agent Full-note and note-search reviews
    met met completed
    not met not_met, done_not_recommended partially_completed, planned, not_done, not_documented
    unknown unknown unclear, error
    not applicable not_applicable applicability not_applicable

    Two rules refine the table:

    • A met or not-met result whose applicability is uncertain is unknown (category_note applicability_uncertain). The documentation fractions leave such results out too.
    • For a step the guideline advises against (SQL agent, recommends_against), a step done anyway (done_not_recommended) is not met. A step not documented as done (not_met) is met (category_note not_recommended_not_done).

    Concordance is met / applicable, where applicable = met + not met. Unknown results are counted and shown, but they are not in the fraction.

    Outputs and metrics¶

    Every file is private (files 0600, folders 0700) and the process umask is 077 while a stage runs.

    File Contents
    item_results.parquet One row per patient and item: provider and department, anchor date, space, item index, name, conditions and category, method, raw status, reason, applicability, category and note, bottom line, query key, and evidence (note id, date, quote, offsets)
    patient_results.parquet, .csv Per patient: items, not applicable, applicable, met, not met, unknown, and concordance (empty when nothing applies)
    provider_summary.csv, department_summary.csv Per group: patients, included, excluded in all and by reason, patients with an applicable item, mean per-patient concordance with a 95% bootstrap interval over patients, the median, and pooled item concordance (met / applicable over every applicable item) with a 95% interval from resampling patients. small_group flags groups under 11 included patients
    provider_items.csv, item_summary.csv Counts and concordance per provider, space and item, and per space and item
    exclusions.parquet Every excluded patient once, at the first stage that excluded it
    flow.json Patients given, anchored, summarized, extracted, matched and assessed, with exclusion reasons at each step
    report.md Counts and metrics only, with the PHI banner and the concordance caveat. Small groups are flagged, not suppressed, since the report is internal
    design_notes.json Method, thresholds, catalogs, models, window and seed, compile totals and gateway usage

    A provider or department is identified as the anchors match it: by ID when the patient row gives one, else by normalized name. The bootstrap intervals use 2,000 draws with seed 42 (evaluation.retro_fidelity.metrics).

    Command line¶

    export MMAI_SITE_CONFIG=/path/to/site.yaml
python -m matchminer_ai.population.review prepare --patients patients.csv --notes notes.parquet \
    --start-date 2025-01-01 --end-date 2026-06-30 \
    --columns notes.provider_id=AUTHOR_ID notes.department_name=DEPARTMENT \
    --output /data/phi/concordance/run1/prepared
python -m matchminer_ai.population.review assess --prepared /data/phi/concordance/run1/prepared \
    --output /data/phi/concordance/run1/assessment

    run does both, into <output>/prepared and <output>/assessment.

    Models. LLMs default to Claude through the site config's Databricks gateway:

    • Haiku 5.5 for summaries, extraction and the note-reading methods;
    • Sonnet 5.5 for compiling the workup queries (the SQL agent's default too).

    --summary-model, --extraction-model, --compile-model and --review-model name another model of the site config's databricks.endpoints (haiku, sonnet, opus) or a full endpoint name. With --<task>-endpoint URL, the task uses that OpenAI-compatible server instead, and the model option names its served model. --thinking on|off sets a local model's thinking switch.

    Other options:

    • --device runs TrialSpace and the decision model (default cpu). The decision model scores each patient's candidate_k spaces, which is slow on a CPU: for a large population use --device cuda with CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES.
    • --concurrency (default 32) bounds requests in flight; the gateway's adaptive limit starts at 32.
    • --query-cache and --embedding-cache share compiled queries and guideline vectors across runs.
    • --max-concurrent-spaces (default 4) assesses several spaces at once.

    Logs and privacy:

    • Token logs. Gateway token counts (never content) go to each stage's gateway_requests.jsonl. Summarize them with python -m matchminer_ai.llm.gateway_usage <log>.
    • Where outputs go. A real run writes only under <data root>/phi or the site config's paths.phi_roots, and never in a no_phi folder. --synthetic (synthetic notes only) allows other folders, but never a phi folder.
    • Logs show counts only.

    matchminer_ai.population.anchors ¶

    Anchor notes of an anchored population review, and each patient's notes through the anchor.

    Each patient row names a provider (by ID and/or name) and optionally a department. The patient's anchor note is a seeded random choice among the notes that provider wrote (in that department), within an optional ascertainment window. Everything the review reads about the patient is then taken from the notes dated on or before the anchor (:func:notes_through_anchor), by any author.

    No model calls; the tables are validated and nothing is written.

    def choose_anchor_notes(patients, notes, *, start_date=None, end_date=None, seed=23, columns=None):
    """Each patient's anchor note: ``(anchors, exclusions)``.

    ``patients`` has one row per patient: ``patient_id``, ``provider_id`` and/or
    ``provider_name`` (at least one per row), and optionally ``department_id`` and/or
    ``department_name``. ``notes`` has ``patient_id``, ``note_date``, ``note_text``,
    optionally ``note_type`` and ``note_id`` (default: the extraction stages' code-derived
    id, so evidence links back), and the note's provider and department fields.
    ``columns`` maps these names to the caller's (``patients.<field>`` or
    ``notes.<field>`` for one table only).

    A note is a candidate when it is the patient's, written by the patient's provider
    (matched on ID when the patient row gives one, otherwise on name, ignoring case and
    spacing), in the patient's department when the row gives one, dated, and within
    ``start_date``-``end_date`` (each optional and inclusive; a bound without a time
    covers its whole day). The anchor is ``random.Random(f"{seed}:{patient_id}")``'s
    choice among the candidates in date and note-ID order, so it does not depend on row
    order.

    ``anchors``: ``patient_id``, the patient row's provider and department fields,
    ``anchor_note_id``, ``anchor_date`` (YYYY-MM-DD), ``anchor_time`` (ISO timestamp,
    None for a date without a time), ``candidate_notes``, ``notes_through_anchor`` and
    ``undated_notes`` (left out). ``exclusions``: ``patient_id``, the same fields,
    ``stage`` (``anchor``) and ``reason`` (a key of ``ANCHOR_REASONS``).
    """
    people = patient_table(patients, columns)
    frame = note_table(notes, columns)
    start = _bound(start_date, "start date")
    end = _bound(end_date, "end date")
    if start is not None and end is not None and start > end:
        raise ValueError("The start date is after the end date.")
    groups = {pid: group for pid, group in frame.groupby("patient_id", sort=False)}
    anchors, exclusions = [], []
    for patient in people.to_dict("records"):
        pid = patient["patient_id"]
        fields = {k: patient[k] for k in GROUP_FIELDS}

        def exclude(reason, pid=pid, fields=fields):
            exclusions.append({"patient_id": pid, **fields, "stage": "anchor", "reason": reason})

        group = groups.get(pid)
        if group is None or group.empty:
            exclude("no_notes")
            continue
        mask = _rows_matching(group, patient, "provider")
        if not mask.any():
            exclude("no_provider_note")
            continue
        department = _rows_matching(group, patient, "department")
        if department is not None:
            mask &= department
            if not mask.any():
                exclude("no_department_note")
                continue
        stamps = group["_timestamp"]
        mask &= stamps.notna()
        if not mask.any():
            exclude("no_dated_note")
            continue
        if start is not None:
            mask &= _not_before(stamps, start)
        if end is not None:
            mask &= _not_after(stamps, end)
        if not mask.any():
            exclude("no_note_in_window")
            continue
        candidates = group.loc[mask].sort_values(["_timestamp", "note_id"], kind="stable")
        chosen = candidates.iloc[random.Random(f"{seed}:{pid}").randrange(len(candidates))]
        stamp = chosen["_timestamp"]
        timed = stamp != stamp.normalize()
        anchors.append({
            "patient_id": pid, **fields,
            "anchor_note_id": chosen["note_id"],
            "anchor_date": stamp.strftime("%Y-%m-%d"),
            "anchor_time": _iso(stamp) if timed else None,
            "candidate_notes": int(len(candidates)),
            "notes_through_anchor": int(through_mask(stamps, stamp).sum()),
            "undated_notes": int(stamps.isna().sum()),
        })
    return (pd.DataFrame(anchors, columns=list(ANCHOR_COLUMNS), dtype=object),
            pd.DataFrame(exclusions, columns=list(EXCLUSION_COLUMNS), dtype=object))

    def notes_through_anchor(notes, anchors, *, columns=None):
    """Every note of each anchored patient, by any author, dated on or before the anchor (inclusive).

    When both the note and the anchor carry a time, timestamps are compared (a note at the
    anchor's timestamp is included); otherwise days are. Undated notes are left out.
    Returns ``patient_id``, ``note_id``, ``note_date`` (YYYY-MM-DD, or an ISO timestamp when the note has a
    time), ``note_type`` and
    ``note_text`` in patient and date order; ``notes`` may already be a :func:`note_table`.
    """
    frame = notes if "_timestamp" in notes else note_table(notes, columns)
    stamps = anchor_timestamps(anchors) if not isinstance(anchors, Mapping) else dict(anchors)
    frame = frame.loc[frame.patient_id.isin(set(stamps))]
    keep = pd.Series(False, index=frame.index)
    for pid, group in frame.groupby("patient_id", sort=False):
        keep.loc[group.index] = through_mask(group["_timestamp"], stamps[pid]).to_numpy()
    out = frame.loc[keep].sort_values(["patient_id", "_timestamp", "note_id"], kind="stable").copy()
    out["note_date"] = [_iso(s) for s in out["_timestamp"]]
    out["note_type"] = [_text(t) for t in out["note_type"]]
    return out[["patient_id", "note_id", "note_date", "note_type", "note_text"]].reset_index(drop=True)

    matchminer_ai.population.prepare ¶

    Part 1 of the anchored concordance review: anchors, then summaries and extraction records through each anchor.

    prepare_anchored_population runs three resumable stages into one folder:

    • anchors/: anchors.parquet, exclusions.parquet and the notes through each anchor (notes.parquet, the only notes the later stages read);
    • summaries/: summarize_patients on those notes (summaries.parquet, qc.parquet);
    • extractions/: extract_patient_records and then extract_document_records linked to them, written as an extraction record store (extractions/store/, matchminer_ai.patients.extraction.store).

    Each stage writes a path-free stage_receipt.json last (matchminer_ai.training.trialspace.stage): its identity holds the input file stamps, the window, the seed, the models and the prompt contracts, never file hashes. A complete stage is skipped, an interrupted one resumes from its checkpoints, and a folder begun under other settings is refused. Every file holds patient data: the process umask is 077 while a stage runs, and a real run writes only under the site's PHI roots (check_output_location).

    def prepare_anchored_population(patients, notes, *, output_dir, start_date=None, end_date=None, seed=23,
                                columns=None, summary_config=None, extraction_config=None,
                                levels="document_and_patient", reuse_documents=None, synthetic=False,
                                check=False, progress_callback=None):
    """Choose anchors, then summarize and extract each patient's notes through the anchor; see the module.

    ``patients`` and ``notes`` are DataFrames or CSV/Parquet paths, as for
    :func:`~matchminer_ai.population.anchors.choose_anchor_notes` (with ``columns``,
    ``start_date``, ``end_date`` and ``seed``). ``summary_config`` sets the ``patient``
    section and ``extraction_config`` the ``document_extraction`` and
    ``patient_extraction`` sections (``levels``: ``document_and_patient``, the default,
    or ``document``). ``reuse_documents``: an extraction checkpoint folder whose
    document records are reused when their input hash and contract match.
    ``synthetic``: the notes are synthetic, so the output may be outside the PHI roots
    (never in a ``phi`` folder). Every note through an anchor is sent to the configured
    endpoints, which must be authorized for the notes. Gateway token logs go to each
    stage's ``gateway_requests.jsonl``.

    Returns the :class:`PreparedPopulation`, or with ``check`` each stage's
    ``(code, reason)`` without running anything.
    """
    from matchminer_ai import load_default_preset
    from matchminer_ai._storage import digest

    if levels not in LEVELS:
        raise ValueError(f"levels must be one of {LEVELS}")
    output = check_output_location(output_dir, synthetic=synthetic)
    progress = progress_callback or (lambda _: None)
    summary_config = summary_config or load_default_preset()
    extraction_config = extraction_config or load_default_preset()
    patient_frame, patient_stamp = _input(patients, "patients", read_table)
    note_frame, note_stamp = _input(notes, "notes", read_table)
    with private_files():
        progress("Choosing anchor notes")
        table = note_table(note_frame, columns)
        anchors, exclusions = choose_anchor_notes(patient_frame, table, start_date=start_date,
                                                  end_date=end_date, seed=seed, columns=columns)
        through = notes_through_anchor(table, anchors)
        del table
        anchor_identity = {
            "version": VERSION,
            "inputs": {"patients": patient_stamp, "notes": note_stamp},
            "start_date": None if start_date is None else str(start_date),
            "end_date": None if end_date is None else str(end_date),
            "seed": seed,
            "columns": dict(sorted((columns or {}).items())),
            "selection_sha256": digest({
                "anchors": anchors[["patient_id", "anchor_note_id", "anchor_date", "anchor_time"]].values.tolist(),
                "exclusions": exclusions[["patient_id", "reason"]].values.tolist(),
                "notes": through[["patient_id", "note_id"]].values.tolist(),
            }),
        }
        statuses = {}
        anchors_dir = output / ANCHORS

        def write_anchors():
            _write_parquet(anchors, anchors_dir / "anchors.parquet")
            _write_parquet(exclusions, anchors_dir / "exclusions.parquet")
            _write_parquet(through, anchors_dir / "notes.parquet")
            return (["anchors.parquet", "exclusions.parquet", "notes.parquet"],
                    {"patients": len(patient_frame), "anchored": len(anchors), "notes": len(through),
                     "exclusions": exclusions.reason.value_counts().to_dict()})

        if check:
            statuses[ANCHORS] = stage_status(anchors_dir, "population-anchors", anchor_identity)
            anchors_id = completed_receipt(anchors_dir, "population-anchors")["receipt_id"] \
                if statuses[ANCHORS][0] == 0 else None
            # A later stage cannot be complete before the anchors are: it waits (10) or must restart (20).
            upstream_status = (statuses[ANCHORS][0], "the anchors stage is not complete"
                               if statuses[ANCHORS][0] == 10 else "the anchors stage differs")
        else:
            anchors_id = run_stage(anchors_dir, "population-anchors", anchor_identity, write_anchors)["receipt_id"]
            progress(f"Anchors: {len(anchors)} of {len(patient_frame)} patients, {len(through)} notes")

        # Summaries -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
        summaries_dir = output / SUMMARIES
        identity = {"version": VERSION, "anchors": anchors_id, "summary": summary_identity(summary_config)}

        def summarize():

            checkpoint = summaries_dir / "checkpoints" / digest(identity)[:16]
            log = summaries_dir / GATEWAY_LOG
            progress(f"Summarizing {through.patient_id.nunique()} patients through their anchors")

                    through[["patient_id", "note_text", "note_date", "note_type"]], config=summary_config,
                    checkpoint_dir=checkpoint, return_metadata=True, return_qc=True)

                result[["patient_id", "cancer_history_summary", "general_exclusion_criteria_evidence"]],
                on="patient_id", how="inner")
            frame["note_count"] = [int(counts.get(p, 0)) for p in frame.patient_id]
            _write_parquet(frame[list(SUMMARY_COLUMNS)], summaries_dir / "summaries.parquet")
            _write_parquet(qc.astype(str), summaries_dir / "qc.parquet")
            return ["summaries.parquet", "qc.parquet"], {
                "anchored": len(anchors), "summarized": len(frame), "gateway_usage": gateway_usage(log)}

        if check:
            statuses[SUMMARIES] = stage_status(summaries_dir, "population-summaries", identity) \
                if anchors_id else upstream_status
        else:
            run_stage(summaries_dir, "population-summaries", identity, summarize, upstream={ANCHORS: anchors_id})

        extractions_dir = output / EXTRACTIONS

                    "extraction": extraction_identity(extraction_config, levels)}

            checkpoint = extractions_dir / "checkpoints"
            checkpoint.mkdir(mode=0o700, parents=True, exist_ok=True)
            reused = 0
            if reuse_documents is not None:
                check_output_location(reuse_documents, synthetic=synthetic)
                reused = _seed_document_checkpoint(reuse_documents, checkpoint, through.note_id)
            log = extractions_dir / GATEWAY_LOG
            patient_records = patient_meta = None
            with gateway_log(log):
                if levels == "document_and_patient":
                    progress(f"Extracting patient records for {through.patient_id.nunique()} patients")
                    patient_records, patient_meta = package.extract_patient_records(
                        through, config=extraction_config, checkpoint_dir=checkpoint, return_metadata=True,
                        progress_callback=lambda done, total: progress(f"Patient records: {done}/{total} chunks"))
                    ids = through.groupby("patient_id").note_id.apply(lambda v: sorted(set(v)))
                    patient_records["note_ids"] = [ids.get(p, []) for p in patient_records.patient_id]
                progress(f"Extracting document records for {len(through)} notes")
                documents, document_meta = package.extract_document_records(
                    through, config=extraction_config, patient_records=patient_records,
                    checkpoint_dir=checkpoint, return_metadata=True,
                    progress_callback=lambda done, total: progress(f"Document records: {done}/{total} notes"))
            model = extraction_config.document_extraction["remote"].get("model_name")
            extraction = extraction_metadata(document_meta, patient_meta, model)
            extraction["gateway_usage"] = gateway_usage(log)
            source = {"kind": "anchored_population", "patients": int(through.patient_id.nunique()),
                      "notes": len(through), "reused_document_checkpoints": reused}
            write_extraction_store(extractions_dir / "store", documents, patient_records,
                                   mode="synthetic" if synthetic else "real", source=source,
                                   extraction=extraction, private=True, overwrite=True)
            names = ["store/manifest.json", "store/document_records.parquet"]
            if patient_records is not None:
                names.append("store/patient_records.parquet")
            statuses_ = {"documents": documents.extraction_status.value_counts().to_dict()}
            if patient_records is not None:
                statuses_["patient_records"] = patient_records.extraction_status.value_counts().to_dict()
            return names, {"statuses": statuses_, "gateway_usage": extraction["gateway_usage"]}

        if check:
            statuses[EXTRACTIONS] = stage_status(extractions_dir, "population-extractions", identity) \
                if anchors_id else upstream_status
            return statuses
        run_stage(extractions_dir, "population-extractions", identity, extract, upstream={ANCHORS: anchors_id})

    def check_output_location(path, *, synthetic):
    """Refuse an output folder on the wrong side of the data: a real run writes notes, summaries and records only
    under the data root's ``phi/`` or a site config ``paths.phi_roots`` folder, never in a ``no_phi`` folder; a
    synthetic run never writes in a ``phi`` folder."""
    from matchminer_ai.site import data_root, load_site_config

    resolved = Path(path).expanduser().resolve()
    parts = resolved.parts
    if synthetic:
        if "phi" in parts:
            raise ValueError("A synthetic run never writes in a phi folder.")
        return resolved
    if "no_phi" in parts:
        raise ValueError("A real run keeps patient data out of no_phi folders.")
    roots = [Path(r).expanduser().resolve() for r in load_site_config().get("paths.phi_roots") or []]
    with contextlib.suppress(ValueError):
        roots.append((data_root() / "phi").resolve())
    if not any(resolved == root or resolved.is_relative_to(root) for root in roots):
        raise ValueError("Refusing to write patient data outside a PHI folder (<data root>/phi or the site "
                         "config's paths.phi_roots); pass synthetic=True only for synthetic notes.")
    return resolved

    matchminer_ai.population.concordance ¶

    Part 2 of the anchored concordance review: each patient's guideline space, then that space's workup items.

    assess_concordance reads part 1's outputs (a folder, or a :class:~.prepare.PreparedPopulation):

    1. Match. retrieve_guideline_considerations ranks every space of the catalogs for each patient summary (TrialSpace top candidate_k, then the decision model's match questions) and keeps the top space. A patient whose top space's P(reasonable) is below min_reasonableness is excluded ("no reasonable guideline match"), keeping that candidate and its scores.
    2. Assess. Patients are grouped by their space, whose workup items (without history-and-physical items) are checked by one method: extraction_queries (default: compiled queries over the extraction records, compiled once per item and cached), extraction_sql (the SQL agent's queries over a database of the run's records), full_notes or note_search (an LLM reads the notes through the anchor). Each patient's evaluation date is the anchor date.
    3. Concordance. Each result maps to met, not met, unknown or not applicable (concordance_category); concordance is met / (met + not met).

    Concordance here is measured from documentation in the notes: a step done but not documented counts as not met.

    def assess_concordance(prepared, *, catalogs, output_dir, method="extraction_queries", min_reasonableness=0.5,
                       candidate_k=20, config=None, compile_config=None, query_cache=None,
                       embedding_cache_dir=None, synthetic=False, max_concurrent_spaces=4,
                       max_concurrent_patients=4, check=False, progress_callback=None):
    """Match each prepared patient to a guideline space, check its workup items and write the concordance report.

    ``prepared``: part 1's folder or :class:`~.prepare.PreparedPopulation` (with
    ``prepared.provenance`` from :func:`~.prepare.load_prepared_population`).
    ``catalogs``: see :func:`catalog_paths`. ``config`` runs TrialSpace and the decision
    model (``embedding``, ``decision_model``) and, for ``full_notes`` and ``note_search``,
    the ``patient`` endpoint. ``compile_config`` writes the queries
    (``workup_query_compile``, or ``workup_sql_agent`` for ``extraction_sql``).
    ``query_cache``: a folder of compiled queries shared across runs (default: the
    output's ``query_cache/``). ``embedding_cache_dir``: guideline vectors (default: the
    output's ``guideline_embeddings/``; guideline text only). Spaces are assessed
    ``max_concurrent_spaces`` at a time. Writes the outputs of
    :func:`~matchminer_ai.population.report.write_concordance_report` into
    ``output_dir`` and returns them (:func:`~matchminer_ai.population.report.read_concordance_report`);
    with ``check``, only each stage's ``(code, reason)``. For the extraction methods, a
    patient without any usable extraction record is excluded (``no_extraction_records``).
    """
    from matchminer_ai import load_default_preset
    from matchminer_ai._storage import QueryCache

    from . import report

    if method not in METHODS:
        raise ValueError(f"method must be one of {METHODS}")
    output = check_output_location(output_dir, synthetic=synthetic)
    if method == "extraction_sql" and query_cache is not None:
        # Accepted SQL entries keep aggregate counts of the records they ran on.
        check_output_location(query_cache, synthetic=synthetic)
    progress = progress_callback or (lambda _: None)
    if not isinstance(prepared, (str, Path)) and not getattr(prepared, "provenance", None):
        raise ValueError("Pass part 1's folder, or a PreparedPopulation from load_prepared_population.")
    if isinstance(prepared, (str, Path)):
        prepared = load_prepared_population(prepared)
    config = config or load_default_preset()
    compile_config = compile_config or config
    paths = catalog_paths(catalogs)
    from matchminer_ai.trials import load_guideline_catalog

    _, catalog_metadata = load_guideline_catalog(paths, return_metadata=True)
    receipts = prepared.provenance["receipts"]
    match_identity = {
        "version": VERSION, "summaries": receipts["summaries"],
        "catalogs": {"sha256": catalog_metadata["catalog_sha256"],
                     "sources": [[s.get("disease"), s.get("version")] for s in catalog_metadata["sources"]]},
        "candidate_k": candidate_k, "min_reasonableness": min_reasonableness, "models": _model_identity(config),
    }
    store = {**(prepared.provenance.get("extraction_store") or {}),
             "model": prepared.provenance.get("extraction_model")}
    extensions = prepared.provenance.get("extensions")
    section = "workup_sql_agent" if method == "extraction_sql" else "workup_query_compile"
    writer = getattr(compile_config, section).get("remote") or {}
    assess_identity = {
        "version": VERSION, "method": method, "extractions": receipts["extractions"],
        "writer": [writer.get("model_name"), compile_config.remote.get("provider")]
        if method.startswith("extraction") else None,
        "reviewer": [config.patient["remote"].get("model_name"), config.remote.get("provider")]
        if not method.startswith("extraction") else None,
    }
    matches_dir = output / MATCHES
    if check:
        statuses = {MATCHES: stage_status(matches_dir, "population-matches", match_identity)}
        code = statuses[MATCHES][0]
        statuses["assessment"] = stage_status(output, "population-concordance", assess_identity) if code == 0 \
            else (code, "the matches stage is not complete" if code == 10 else "the matches stage differs")
        return statuses
    with private_files():
        def match():
            progress(f"Matching {len(prepared.summaries)} patients to guideline spaces")
            matches, spaces, metadata = match_guideline_spaces(
                prepared.summaries, paths, config=config, candidate_k=candidate_k,
                min_reasonableness=min_reasonableness,
                embedding_cache_dir=embedding_cache_dir or output / "guideline_embeddings",
                progress_callback=progress)
            matches.to_parquet(matches_dir / "guideline_matches.parquet", index=False)
            (matches_dir / "spaces.json").write_text(json.dumps(spaces, ensure_ascii=False))
            return ["guideline_matches.parquet", "spaces.json"], {
                "matched": int((~matches.excluded).sum()), "excluded": int(matches.excluded.sum()),
                "model_metadata": metadata.get("model_metadata"), "retrieval": metadata.get("retrieval")}

        match_receipt = run_stage(matches_dir, "population-matches", match_identity, match,
                                  upstream={"summaries": receipts["summaries"]})
        matches = pd.read_parquet(matches_dir / "guideline_matches.parquet")
        spaces = json.loads((matches_dir / "spaces.json").read_text())

        def assess():
            from matchminer_ai.patients.workup import without_history_and_physical

            fields = prepared.anchors.set_index("patient_id")[list(GROUP_FIELDS)].to_dict("index")
            missing = report.patients_without_records(prepared) if method.startswith("extraction") else set()
            included = matches.loc[~matches.excluded & ~matches.patient_id.isin(missing)]
            groups = {sid: group.patient_id.tolist() for sid, group in included.groupby("space_trial_id",
                                                                                          sort=True)}
            no_workup = [sid for sid in groups if not without_history_and_physical(spaces[sid])["diagnostic_workup"]]
            cache = _writer_cache(query_cache or output / "query_cache", compile_config, section)
            sql_database = sql_cache = None
            if method == "extraction_sql":
                from matchminer_ai.patients import build_extraction_database, write_extraction_database

                progress("Building the SQL agent's exploration database from the run's records")
                frames = [f for f in (prepared.documents, prepared.patient_records) if f is not None]
                frames = [f.drop(columns=[c for c in ("note_ids",) if c in f]) for f in frames]
                sql_database = write_extraction_database(output / "sql" / "exploration.duckdb",
                                                         build_extraction_database(frames), private=True)
                sql_cache = QueryCache(Path(query_cache or output / "query_cache") / "workup_sql")
            todo = [sid for sid in groups if sid not in no_workup]
            done = {"spaces": 0}

            def one(sid):
                patients, queries = assess_space(
                    spaces[sid], groups[sid], prepared, method=method, config=config,
                    compile_config=compile_config, query_cache=cache, sql_database=sql_database,
                    sql_cache=sql_cache, extensions=extensions, max_concurrent_patients=max_concurrent_patients)
                done["spaces"] += 1
                progress(f"Assessed {done['spaces']}/{len(todo)} guideline spaces")
                return sid, patients, queries

            log = output / "gateway_requests.jsonl"
            rows, compile_summary = [], {}
            with gateway_log(log), ThreadPoolExecutor(max_workers=max(1, max_concurrent_spaces)) as pool:
                for sid, patients, queries in pool.map(one, todo):
                    rows.extend(_item_rows(patients, without_history_and_physical(spaces[sid]), method, fields))
                    if queries:
                        compile_summary[sid] = queries
            items = pd.DataFrame(rows)
            exclusions = report.all_exclusions(prepared, matches, missing, {
                pid for sid in no_workup for pid in groups[sid]})
            tables = report.write_concordance_report(
                output, items=items, matches=matches, prepared=prepared, exclusions=exclusions,
                design={"method": method, "min_reasonableness": min_reasonableness, "candidate_k": candidate_k,
                        "catalogs": [[s.get("disease"), s.get("title"), s.get("version")]
                                     for s in catalog_metadata["sources"]],
                        "models": _model_identity(config), "writer": assess_identity["writer"],
                        "reviewer": assess_identity["reviewer"], "extraction_store": store,
                        "compile": _compile_totals(compile_summary), "gateway_usage": gateway_usage(log),
                        "prepared": prepared.provenance.get("identity", {}).get("anchors"),
                        "prepared_receipts": receipts, "match_receipt": match_receipt["receipt_id"],
                        "matching": match_receipt.get("content", {}).get("retrieval")})

    def concordance_category(status, applicability, *, recommends_against=False):
    """``(category, note)`` of one workup result.

    - **Not applicable:** status ``not_applicable`` or applicability ``not_applicable``.
    - **Unknown:** ``unknown``, ``unclear`` or ``error``; also a met or not-met result whose applicability is
      uncertain (note ``applicability_uncertain``), which stays out of the fraction as the documentation fractions
      do.
    - **Met:** ``met`` or ``completed``; for a step the guideline advises against, a ``not_met`` result (the step
      is not documented as done; note ``not_recommended_not_done``).
    - **Not met:** ``not_met``, ``partially_completed``, ``planned``, ``not_done``, ``not_documented``, and
      ``done_not_recommended`` (a step advised against, done anyway).
    """
    if status not in STATUS_CATEGORIES:
        raise ValueError(f"Unknown workup status {status!r}.")
    category = STATUS_CATEGORIES[status]
    if category == "not_applicable" or applicability == "not_applicable":
        return "not_applicable", None
    if category in ("met", "not_met") and applicability != "applies":
        return "unknown", "applicability_uncertain"
    if recommends_against and status == "not_met":
        return "met", "not_recommended_not_done"
    return category, None

    def catalog_paths(catalogs):
    """Catalog folders: ``catalogs`` is a catalog root (the latest complete, audited catalog of each disease,
    ``latest_guideline_catalogs``), one catalog folder or ``paradigms.jsonl``, or a list of them."""
    from matchminer_ai.trials.guideline_catalog import latest_guideline_catalogs

    if isinstance(catalogs, (str, Path)):
        root = Path(catalogs)
        if root.suffix == ".jsonl" or (root / "paradigms.jsonl").is_file():
            return [str(root)]
        rows = latest_guideline_catalogs(root)
        if not rows:
            raise ValueError("No complete, audited guideline catalogs under the catalog root.")
        return [row["path"] for row in rows]
    paths = [str(Path(c)) for c in catalogs]
    if not paths:
        raise ValueError("Supply at least one guideline catalog.")
    return paths

    def write_concordance_report(output, *, items, matches, prepared, exclusions, design):
    """Write every output (``OUTPUTS``) into ``output``; returns ``{"outputs", "flow"}``."""
    from .concordance import CATEGORIES, STATUS_CATEGORIES

    output = Path(output)
    items = items.reindex(columns=list(ITEM_COLUMNS)) if len(items) else pd.DataFrame(columns=list(ITEM_COLUMNS))
    if len(items):
        items["anchor_date"] = items.patient_id.map(prepared.anchors.set_index("patient_id").anchor_date)
    people = pd.concat([prepared.anchors[["patient_id", *GROUP_FIELDS]],
                        prepared.exclusions[["patient_id", *GROUP_FIELDS]]], ignore_index=True)
    patients = patient_results(items, matches, prepared.anchors)
    providers = group_summary(people, patients, items, exclusions, PROVIDER)
    departments = group_summary(people, patients, items, exclusions, DEPARTMENT)
    space_items = ("space_trial_id", "space_name", "item_index", "item_name")
    provider_items = item_rates(items, space_items, by=PROVIDER)
    item_summary = item_rates(items, space_items)
    flow = flow_counts(people, exclusions)
    overall = concordance_metrics(patients, items)
    synthetic = (prepared.provenance.get("extraction_store") or {}).get("mode") == "synthetic"
    notes = {**design, "created_utc": datetime.now(timezone.utc).isoformat(timespec="seconds"),
             "synthetic": synthetic, "categories": list(CATEGORIES), "status_categories": STATUS_CATEGORIES,
             "concordance": "met / (met + not met) per patient; unknown and not applicable stay out",
             "small_group": SMALL_GROUP, "overall": overall}
    _write(output / "item_results.parquet", _parquet(items))
    _write(output / "patient_results.parquet", _parquet(patients))
    _write(output / "patient_results.csv", patients.to_csv(index=False))
    _write(output / "provider_summary.csv", providers.to_csv(index=False))
    _write(output / "department_summary.csv", departments.to_csv(index=False))
    _write(output / "provider_items.csv", provider_items.to_csv(index=False))
    _write(output / "item_summary.csv", item_summary.to_csv(index=False))

    _write(output / "flow.json", json.dumps(flow, indent=1))
    _write(output / "design_notes.json", json.dumps(notes, indent=1, default=str))
    _write(output / "report.md", render_report(flow, overall, providers, departments, item_summary, design,
                                               synthetic=synthetic))
    return {"outputs": list(OUTPUTS), "flow": flow}

    def read_concordance_report(folder):
    """The written tables (DataFrames by name) and ``flow``."""
    folder = Path(folder)

                    ("item_results.parquet", "patient_results.parquet", "exclusions.parquet")}
